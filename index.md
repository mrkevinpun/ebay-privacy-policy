# Privacy Policy – Kevin Pun eBay Purchase Tracker

Last updated: October 7, 2026

## Purpose

Kevin Pun eBay Purchase Tracker is a personal-use application that connects to eBay through eBay's authorized APIs and OAuth authentication.

## Information Accessed

The application may access information made available by eBay through the permissions granted by the authorized eBay account.

The application is intended solely for personal use by the account owner.

## Use of Information

Information obtained through eBay is used only to provide the application's personal tracking and analysis functionality.

The application does not sell, rent, or share eBay user information with third parties.

## Data Storage

The application does not maintain a persistent database of eBay user data. Information obtained through eBay APIs is accessed as needed for the application's personal-use functionality.

## Authentication

Authentication is handled through eBay OAuth. The application does not collect or store the user's eBay password.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality or data-handling practices of the application change.

## Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the application owner.
